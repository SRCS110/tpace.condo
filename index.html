Surfline 8th Avenue So. is a 3rd-floor oceanfront condo in the Sandpiper building — one of Jacksonville Beach's most coveted addresses. The living room and front bedroom look directly over the dunes and Atlantic, while the private balcony is the perfect perch to watch the sun rise out of the ocean each morning.

Start and end each day on the beachfront balcony. The updated kitchen — all-new counters, full appliances, dishwasher — makes cooking in effortless. Sleep soundly with central A/C and Hungarian goose-down pillows. Just 8 blocks from downtown Jax Beach.
